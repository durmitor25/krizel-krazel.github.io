About

Photography built on patience and contrast.

TABAK is the studio practice of a professional photographer working across editorial commissions, remote landscapes, and intimate portraiture. The work is led by light first — high contrast, deep blacks, and the kind of quiet that holds a viewer in place.

Every project begins with research and ends with restraint. No filler frames, no visual noise. Just the image, the paper, and the story it carries.

Based between assignments, available worldwide for editorial, commercial, and private portrait work.
